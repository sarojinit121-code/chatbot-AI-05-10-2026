வணக்கம்! **Ooty** உங்கள் பயணத்திற்கு மிகச் சிறந்த தேர்வு. உங்களுக்கான பிரத்யேக தகவல்கள் கீழே:

#### 🗓️ 2-Day Classic Ooty Nature, Hills & Tea Experience
*Perfect 2-day getaway to the Queen of Hill Stations. Balances cool heights, fragrant tea factories, and serene boat cruises.*

---
##### 📍 **Day 1: Iconic Peaks, Tea Aromas & Botanical Splendor**
- 🌅 **Morning:** Start at 8:30 AM at Doddabetta Peak for crisp valley views from the Telescope House before mist rolls in. Visit the adjacent Doddabetta Tea Factory & Museum for live tea-making and hot spiced tea.
- ☀️ **Afternoon:** Head down to Government Botanical Garden at 1:00 PM. Stroll through the 20-million-year-old fossil tree and Italian garden. Have lunch with authentic Chettinad/South Indian meals in Ooty town.
- 🌆 **Evening:** At 4:00 PM, reach Ooty Lake & Boat House. Enjoy pedal boating under the eucalyptus trees, followed by shopping for homemade chocolates on Commercial Road.
- 🍽️ **Food Pick:** Grab freshly made hazelnut dark chocolates at King Star or Moddy's Confectionery.

---
##### 📍 **Day 2: Toy Train Heritage, Rose Cascades & Pykara Waterfalls**
- 🌅 **Morning:** Board the famous Nilgiri Mountain Railway (Toy Train) from Ooty to Coonoor or visit the Government Rose Garden with 20,000+ vibrant blooms across terraced hills.
- ☀️ **Afternoon:** Drive 20 km out towards Pykara Lake. Enjoy lunch at the Pykara lake boathouse restaurant surrounded by pine forests.
- 🌆 **Evening:** Take a thrilling speedboat ride on Pykara Lake and witness the roaring Pykara Waterfalls. Head back to Ooty admiring sunset reflections over Wenlock Downs (9th Mile shooting point).
- 🍽️ **Food Pick:** Savor hot bajjis with Nilgiri masala chai beside Pykara Lake.

💰 **Estimated Budget:** `₹3,500 - ₹6,000 per person (excluding stay)`
💡 **Pro-Tip:** Book Toy Train tickets on IRCTC 30 days in advance. Carry a warm jacket as evenings drop below 12°C.

#### 🏛️ Top Places & Attractions in Ooty:
**1. Doddabetta Peak** (Viewpoint & Hills) `[Must-Visit Peak]`
   - 📖 *The highest peak in the Nilgiri Hills at 2,637 meters (8,650 ft). Offers an awe-inspiring panoramic 360-degree vista of the Nilgiris, Coimbatore plains, and Mysore plateau.*
   - ⏰ **Timings:** 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
   - 🎫 **Entry Fee:** ₹10 per person, ₹50 for camera
   - ✨ **Highlights:** Telescope House, 360-degree Nilgiri Panorama, Shola Forest canopy, Chilly mountain breeze
   - 🚗 **Route:** Located 9 km from Ooty town center. Easily reached by local government buses from Ooty bus stand (Route 11) or rental taxi/auto (approx ₹300-₹400 round trip). Nearest airport: Coimbatore (88 km).
   - 🍲 **Famous Food Nearby:** Hot Cardamom Chai, Steaming Vadas, Roasted Sweet Corn at summit

**2. Government Botanical Garden** (Nature & Botanical) `[Top Attraction]`
   - 📖 *Spread over 55 acres on the lower slopes of Doddabetta peak, housing over 650 species of exotic and indigenous plants, a 20-million-year-old fossilized tree trunk, and terraced Italian gardens.*
   - ⏰ **Timings:** 7:00 AM - 6:30 PM
   - 🎫 **Entry Fee:** ₹40 (Adults), ₹20 (Kids), Camera ₹50
   - ✨ **Highlights:** 20-Million-Year-Old Fossil Tree, Fern House, Italian Lawns, Toda Hill, Orchidarium
   - 🚗 **Route:** Just 2.5 km from Ooty Railway Station & Bus Stand. Frequent auto-rickshaws (₹80-₹100) and town buses connect directly to the main gate.
   - 🍲 **Famous Food Nearby:** Ooty Homemade Chocolates, Eucalyptus oil products, Fresh strawberries

**3. Ooty Lake & Boat House** (Boating & Leisure) `[Family Favorite]`
   - 📖 *An iconic 65-acre artificial lake constructed in 1824 by John Sullivan, surrounded by whispering eucalyptus groves. Features paddle, rowing, and motor boat cruises, plus a mini train for kids.*
   - ⏰ **Timings:** 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
   - 🎫 **Entry Fee:** ₹15 (Entry), Boating ₹180 - ₹450
   - ✨ **Highlights:** Row & Pedal Boating, Motor Safari Boat, Lakeside Cycling, Mini Toy Train for Kids, Horse Riding
   - 🚗 **Route:** Only 1.5 km from Ooty Bus Stand and Ooty Railway Station. Within walking distance or a 5-minute auto ride (₹60).
   - 🍲 **Famous Food Nearby:** Nilgiri Fresh Strawberries with cream, Hot Molaga Bajjis, Chocolate Fudge

**4. Government Rose Garden** (Floral & Leisure) `[Scenic & Romantic]`
   - 📖 *One of the largest rose gardens in India situated on Elk Hill slopes at 2,200m, featuring over 20,000 rose varieties across 5 curving terraces.*
   - ⏰ **Timings:** 8:30 AM - 6:00 PM
   - 🎫 **Entry Fee:** ₹40 (Adults), ₹20 (Kids), Camera ₹50
   - ✨ **Highlights:** Over 2,800 cultivars of roses, Miniature roses, Rare green and black roses, Nila Maadam panoramic observation platform
   - 🚗 **Route:** Located 3 km from the Ooty bus stand on the slopes of Elk Hill. Quick auto ride (₹80) from town center.
   - 🍲 **Famous Food Nearby:** Natural rose water, Rose gulkand, Herbal tea

**5. Pykara Lake & Waterfalls** (Waterfalls & Nature) `[Adventure & Nature]`
   - 📖 *Scenic river and lake nestled 21 km from Ooty amidst pristine shola forests and Toda tribal homeland. Features majestic cascades breaking into twin falls and high-speed boating.*
   - ⏰ **Timings:** 9:30 AM - 5:30 PM
   - 🎫 **Entry Fee:** ₹10 (Entry), Speed Boating ₹800 (for 8 people)
   - ✨ **Highlights:** Twin Cascading Falls, Forest Speedboat Cruise, Dense Pine Woods, Wenlock Downs backdrop
   - 🚗 **Route:** Located 21 km from Ooty along the Ooty-Mysore highway. Accessible via state transport buses heading to Gudalur/Mysore or hire cab (₹1200 round trip including waiting).
   - 🍲 **Famous Food Nearby:** Hot Maggi noodles, Spiced tea, Steamed sweet corn

**6. Nilgiri Mountain Railway (Toy Train)** (Heritage & Railway) `[UNESCO Heritage]`
   - 📖 *UNESCO World Heritage toy train built in 1908 connecting Mettupalayam to Ooty via Coonoor. Uses an ingenious rack-and-pinion system through 16 tunnels and 250 bridges.*
   - ⏰ **Timings:** Departures: 9:15 AM & 2:00 PM from Ooty
   - 🎫 **Entry Fee:** ₹30 (Second Class) to ₹205 (First Class)
   - ✨ **Highlights:** Vintage steam locomotive, Deep Nilgiri gorges, Lush tea valleys, Historic Swiss-engineered cog wheels
   - 🚗 **Route:** Departs from Udagamandalam (Ooty) Railway Station located in town center. Connected by rail to Mettupalayam and Coonoor.
   - 🍲 **Famous Food Nearby:** Nilgiri spiced vadas and tea sold on station platforms
